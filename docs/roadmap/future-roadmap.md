# Future product enhancements

## Review Comments / Q&A — deferred, not rejected

Revisit after the Review system and Helpful interactions are stable; not a Phase 11 requirement or a pre-launch blocker.

- One discussion attached to the root continuous Review, not separate Timeline Update threads.
- Comments and one-level replies; full conversation primarily in Review Details.
- Compact discussion access/count on cards.
- Reuse Experience discussion infrastructure where appropriate, without assuming identical data models or permissions.
- Parent-review Public/Private inheritance, authenticated authorization, moderation, reporting, blocking, notifications and deletion/retention rules must be designed together.
- Do not create review-comment tables or change existing Experience comments during Phase 11.

Phase 12 remains structured aggregation, minimum sample thresholds and entity signals. Personalized ranking and journey similarity are separate proposals.
